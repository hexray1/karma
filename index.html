The Shift
Labor's share of income is at the lowest level ever recorded. AI isn't the threat to this trend — it is the trend.

For decades, workers captured a shrinking cut of what the economy produces — from ~65% after WWII to ~52% today. Economists now name the cause plainly: technology, with AI as the latest and fastest force. Every point of share that moves from labor to capital is a point of the $66T pool up for conversion. The direction is one-way. The question was never if. Only who builds the rails.
